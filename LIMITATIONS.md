# Limitations

The observed result is a small aggregate directional asymmetry in this fixed test set. The following limits constrain measurement confidence and generalization.

- **Ten scenarios (N=10), two repeats per condition.** The low-resolution scenario-bootstrap CI describes uncertainty across this small empirical scenario set.
- **Constructed English scenarios and quota-constrained selection.** They are not a probability sample of relationship conflicts, languages or populations. Both character genders swap, so violator- and target-gender effects cannot be separated.
- **One requested model/configuration/time period.** The study requested GPT-6 Astra/medium in September 2026; the backend did not expose independently resolved model/effort metadata. +0.20 is not a universal effect for all LLMs or later versions.
- **LLM-based measurement.** Scores came from GPT-5.6 Sol, not an independent human panel. All40 scores and the ten-run Judge 2 repeat audit were produced within the same overall session. The audit is not fully independent judge validation; shared context, anchoring and judge bias remain possible. Nine exact repeat agreements do not resolve those risks.
- **Two collection batches.** Batch is confounded with scenario identity. Between-batch differences cannot be separated from differences in scenario content.
- **Composite and secondary endpoints.** AIx weights and magnitude thresholds were frozen, but they are not psychometric calibration. Secondary dimension differences are descriptive; no formal subgroup significance testing was performed. A zero composite delta does not prove equal wording or all equal dimension scores.
- **Mechanism and downstream impact.** The dataset does not identify a training/alignment cause, model intent or causal effects on human users. Similar average response lengths do not by themselves rule out length-related confounding.
- **Observable isolation.** Recorded telemetry supports fresh contexts and zero tools; hidden runtime system context and OS-wide file activity were not fully exposed. Internal session dumps are withheld from the public copy for privacy.
- **Recovery and reproduction.** Internal historical hashes and archive members passed. The deleted pre-recovery filesystem cannot be compared. The original analysis implementation/resampling stream was not in the analysis archive; the published verification implementation is a transparent reconstruction of the frozen arithmetic, not a recovered original script.
- **Illustrative example selection.** README excerpts come from S03, already identified as the strongest scenario, and are not a representative random sample. Full responses and scores are available for review.

Direction and magnitude remain separate: seven positive, three exactly zero, no negative; seven near-equivalent, two small, one moderate, no large. Four positive deltas are within the near-equivalence threshold.
